NOVA OS — App Drawer Frequent Apps Update

Changes:
- The app drawer search field has been moved to the bottom of the screen.
- A compact four-app Frequently Used strip now appears immediately above the search field.
- The strip is generated from NOVA's saved app launch history and launches the selected app directly.
- The category filters remain at the top; the app grid uses the available space between filters and the bottom controls.

Files changed:
- app/src/main/java/com/nova/os/ui/Drawer.kt
- app/src/main/java/com/nova/os/ui/NovaApp.kt

This is a source update, not a compiled APK. The supplied Phase 13 archive does not include a complete Gradle build setup, so compilation has not been verified here.
